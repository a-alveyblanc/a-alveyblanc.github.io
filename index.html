Addison Alvey-Blanco

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I am advised by Andreas Klöckner. My research interests include GPU programming, high-performance kernels, compiler transformations, and low-level GPU code generation, including PTX and SASS.

I am interested in systems that give application engineers, performance engineers, and compiler engineers appropriate levels of abstraction for expressing and optimizing computations, such as through scheduling languages.

If I had more time, I would spend it learning more about randomized numerical linear algebra.
